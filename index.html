Hi! I'm John Carlo N. Abiva, a dedicated 2D game developer and digital artist. I bridge the gap between creative visual design and responsive game architecture, transforming rough conceptual doodles into living, breathing game worlds.

For Game Development, I engineer responsive game loops, state machines, and combat systems using Godot Engine (4.x / 3.x) and Unity 2D.

For 2D Art & Animation, my pipeline utilizes Ibis Paint for ideation and speed-sketches, Clip Studio Paint for detailed character concept turnarounds and cel shading, Pixel Studio for frame-by-frame sprites and tilesets, and Krita for textured environmental matte art. For 3D Modeling, I build low-poly models and environmental props in Blender.
